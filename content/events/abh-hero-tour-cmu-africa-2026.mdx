## What happened

On **Tuesday, 3 November 2026**, **Africa's Business Heroes (ABH)** brought its Hero Tour to **Carnegie Mellon University Africa** for an afternoon celebrating the entrepreneurs shaping Africa's future.

The student engagement session connected CMU-Africa's emerging founders with entrepreneurial experience and created a platform for students to present the businesses and ideas they are building.

## Student pitches and practical feedback

CMU-Africa students took the floor to share their ventures, explain the problems they are tackling, and walk the audience through their solutions and plans for growth. The pitch session gave participants an opportunity to:

- Make their ventures and brands visible
- Receive practical feedback on their ideas and approach
- Learn from an experienced entrepreneur's journey
- Gain insight into navigating challenges and identifying next steps

## Event details

- **Date:** Tuesday, 3 November 2026
- **Time:** 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM
- **Venue:** CMU-Africa Campus, Kigali

The Hero Tour reflected a shared commitment by ABH, CMU-Africa, and the Africa Innovators Society to help promising student founders strengthen their ideas and build businesses that can shape Africa's future.
