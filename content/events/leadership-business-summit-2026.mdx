## About the summit

On **Saturday, 29 August 2026**, AIS hosts the **Leadership & Business Summit 2026** at **Carnegie Mellon University Africa** under the theme **"THE NEXT FOUNDERS: From Vision to Venture in Africa's Innovation Economy."**

Registration opens at **11:00 AM**. The programme runs **11:30 AM to 3:00 PM**, followed by a networking lunch until **3:45 PM**.

### Keynote speakers

- **Prof. João Barros**, President of the National Agency for Research and Innovation
- **Oluwaseun David Adepoju, PhD**, Managing Partner, CcHUB/iHUB
- **Prof. Keith Straughan**, CEO, Imcon Consulting

### Panel 1, The First Mile: Turning an Idea into Something Real

- **Cynthia Iradukunda**, CEO, Safe Speak
- **Victor Olufemi**, CEO, Lyngua Labs
- **Tobi Omotosho**, Co-founder, moniepal
- **Charity Ineza**, CEO, Turiabagara Limited

### Panel 2, Leading Through Growth: People, Capital, and Staying Power

- **David Masoperh**, Co-founder, Pebia
- **Prof. Jesse Thornburg**, CEO, Grid Fruits
- **Gabriel Ekman**, Co-founder, BAG INNOVATION
- **Vivens Uwizeyimana**, CEO and Founder, Umurova

### Student builders showcase

- **Frank Ebeledike**, Co-founder, Exuar
- **Paul Mugume**, Co-founder, Vitalabs
