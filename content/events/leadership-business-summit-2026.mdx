## About the summit

On **Saturday, 29 August 2026**, AIS hosts the **Leadership & Business Summit 2026** at **Carnegie Mellon University Africa** under the theme **"The Next Founders: From Vision to Venture in Africa's Innovation Economy."**

The Summit walks the full founder journey in a single programme: keynote addresses from leaders who have built ventures and ecosystems, panels of emerging innovators and CEOs, and a showcase of CMU-Africa students presenting the ventures they are building right now.

Registration opens at **11:00 AM**. The programme runs **11:30 AM to 3:00 PM**, followed by a networking lunch until **3:45 PM**.

**Free attendance. Registration required. Open to students, young professionals, and the Kigali innovation community.**
