## What happened

On **Saturday, 29 August 2026**, AIS hosted the **Leadership & Business Summit 2026** at **Carnegie Mellon University Africa** under the theme **"The Next Founders: From Vision to Venture in Africa's Innovation Economy."**

The half-day programme walked the founder journey in one sitting: keynotes from leaders who have built ventures and ecosystems, panels of emerging innovators and CEOs, and a showcase of CMU-Africa students presenting the ventures they are building now.

Registration opened at **11:00 AM**. The programme ran **11:30 AM to 3:00 PM**, followed by a networking lunch until **3:45 PM**. Students, young professionals, and the Kigali innovation community filled the room.
